# Privacy Policy

**FormReady — Exam Photo & Sign**
Last updated: 17 September 2026

## The short version

FormReady does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data. Your
photos, signatures and documents never leave your phone.

The app has no servers. It does not hold the `INTERNET` permission, so it is not
technically capable of sending your files anywhere, whether or not you are
online.

## What the app does with your files

When you open a photo, signature or PDF in FormReady, it is read from your
device, processed in the app's memory on your device, and written back to your
device when you choose to save or share it.

- Nothing is uploaded to any server.
- Nothing is copied to any location you did not choose.
- Nothing is retained by the app after you leave a tool.

## Data we collect

None.

FormReady contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking
identifiers, and no accounts. There is nothing to sign in to, and no profile is
created. We cannot see which exams you prepare for, which files you open, or
that you use the app at all.

## Permissions, and why each one exists

FormReady requests three Android permissions. All three serve one feature —
compressing a large PDF while the app is in the background — and none of them
gives the app access to the internet or to your data.

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To show you a notification when a long compression finishes, so you do not have to sit and watch the screen. Declining it only means no notification; compression still works. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Android stops an app's work seconds after you switch away from it. This lets a compression that is already running continue while you use your phone for something else. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` | The category Android requires an app to declare for that kind of ongoing work. |

FormReady does **not** request internet access, location, contacts, microphone,
camera-roll-wide storage access, or any other permission.

When you pick a photo or take one, Android's own picker or camera hands the app
that single file. The app never browses your gallery.

## Android's own backup

If you have Android's backup turned on for your phone, Android — not FormReady —
copies app settings to your own Google account so they can be restored on a new
phone. For FormReady that covers two things: whether you have seen the welcome
screen, and any exam presets you have saved yourself.

Your photos, signatures and documents are **not** included. Files the app
prepares are written to its cache, which Android's backup never copies, and
files you save go where you chose to save them.

## Children

FormReady is a utility for preparing documents for examination and recruitment
forms. It contains no user-generated content, no communication features, and no
advertising, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.
