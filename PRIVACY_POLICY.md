# FormReady Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

FormReady ("the app") helps students, teachers, and job applicants prepare
PDFs, photos, and signatures for official forms — entirely on your device.

## Data collection

FormReady does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has
no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no account system, and no network
backend. Every document, photo, and signature you process stays on your
device, inside the app's private storage.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — used only when you tap "Take photo" (for example, to
  photograph a paper signature or capture a document page). The photo is
  processed locally and is never uploaded anywhere. You can deny this
  permission and use the gallery picker instead.
- **Photos/media (gallery access)** — used only when you choose a file
  through Android's built-in photo/file picker. FormReady never scans your
  gallery or files on its own; it only reads the specific file you select.

## Storage and sharing

Processed files (compressed/merged/split PDFs, resized or biometric photos,
prepared signatures) are written to the app's private storage. They are only
shared outside the app when you explicitly tap "Share" or "Open," which hands
the file to an app you choose via Android's standard share sheet — FormReady
itself never sends your files anywhere.

## Children's privacy

FormReady does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children,
because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same
location with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the support
channel listed on the app's Play Store listing.
